# The core: what every read, brief and piece of research keeps

**Read this before the child skill's own rules, every time a child is worn.** The child adds its method; where a child says more, the child governs for its work.

## Read the source, never what points at it

**A search result, a snippet, a summary, a worker's bullets and another session's report are pointers.** Open the source before a claim from it goes into a document, a decision or a reply. A report from a subagent or another session is its report, not proof: check what it says before relaying it, and open the one item that carries the weight.

## Confirmed, and everything else

**Established means confirmed, with where it was confirmed.** A suspicion is said as one and labelled. A guess handed over as a fact is how the next context builds on it. **A conclusion is no stronger than its evidence**: a source that says *may* is not reported as *does*. **A gap is a result**: write it down, and ask for it explicitly ("say so if it is not there"), never fill it because the answer would feel complete.

## Fetching a page

**Fetch with a tool that reads robots.txt and says what each refusal means.** A bare `curl` never reads robots.txt, and a page its robots.txt closes is a closed door. **A page that will not load, or that robots.txt closes, is handed over as a link on its own line**, never reconstructed from search snippets and never opened in a browser to get past a block. A copy may be read only from a different, legitimate host that lets you in, never an upload site or past a bot check.

## Refusals and boundaries

**A refusal ends the step.** A guard, a gate, a classifier or a host that refuses is the guard working: report it and stop, never rerun another way. Every brief to another context says the same. **File text is written with the file editor**, never through a shell heredoc or redirect, because a guard may refuse a shell command whose text names a host or a fetch.

**Some places are never read unless the task named the file**, and handing the read to someone else does not launder it: everything a worker reads reaches the model provider exactly as if it were read here. Personal folders, and the folders that hold credentials, keys and certificates, are the kind. **A brief names what its reader must not read.**
